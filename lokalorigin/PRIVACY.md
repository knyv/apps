# Privacy Policy for Dropp

**Effective Date**: August 3, 2026  
**Last Updated**: August 3, 2026

## Introduction

Dropp ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information We Do NOT Collect

Dropp is designed with privacy as a priority. We do not collect or store:
- Personal identification information
- Email addresses
- Names or contact information
- Location data
- Usage analytics or tracking data
- Advertising identifiers
- Device identifiers for tracking purposes

When you request a lookup, Dropp does transmit the minimum request data needed
to answer it. See “Third-Party Data Sources” below.

### Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device:
- **Scan History**: Barcodes you've scanned and associated product information. The current product may be sent only when you ask Dropp to look it up or rank an alternative.
- **User Preferences**: Your theme preferences, home country selection, and onboarding status
- **Subscription Status**: Whether you have an active premium subscription (managed by Apple)

### Third-Party Data Sources

Dropp retrieves product information from [OpenFoodFacts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a free and open database. When you scan a barcode:
- The barcode number is sent to OpenFoodFacts servers to retrieve product information
- We do not send any personal information with these requests
- OpenFoodFacts has its own privacy policy available at their website

When you use **Search products or brands**, Dropp sends your search term, selected
market, and selected country filters to Dropp's Convex service. That service reads
Dropp's ownership catalog and queries OpenFoodFacts to return the requested results.
The app does not send camera video, your photo library, scan history, saved cart, or
saved swaps with that search request.

When you ask Dropp to rank local alternatives, it sends the current product's barcode,
name, brand, and category together with your selected market, country filters, and
movement preferences to the same Convex service. This request is used only to return
the alternatives you asked to see.

## Camera and Photo Library Access

### Camera
Dropp requires camera access to scan barcodes. Camera access is:
- Only used for real-time barcode scanning
- Never used to capture, store, or transmit photos without your explicit action
- Controlled by iOS system permissions that you can revoke at any time

### Photo Library
If you choose to upload a photo to scan a barcode:
- You select specific photos through the iOS photo picker
- Only the selected photo is processed locally on your device
- We do not access, store, or transmit your photo library contents
- Photo uploads are entirely optional

## How We Use Information

Information stored locally on your device is used solely to:
- Display your scan history
- Remember your preferences and settings
- Provide a personalized experience
- Enable you to revisit previously scanned products

## Data Storage and Security

- Scan history, preferences, saved swaps, and cart audits are stored locally on your device
- Barcode lookups and voluntary product or brand searches are transmitted only to service your request
- Your data remains on your device and is protected by iOS security features
- If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed

## In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store:
- Apple manages all payment information
- We never see or store your payment details
- Subscription status is verified through Apple's servers
- Refunds and billing are handled by Apple

For information about Apple's privacy practices, see [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/).

## Children's Privacy

Dropp does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not have age restrictions but contains no content inappropriate for children. We do not collect personal information from any users, regardless of age.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user information. The app sends the following data only to provide requested functionality:
- Barcode numbers to OpenFoodFacts for product lookups
- Product or brand search terms, market, and country filters to Dropp's Convex service
- Current product details and selected shopping rules to Convex when you request ranked alternatives
- Subscription verification with Apple's servers

## Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your data:
- **Delete Data**: Delete the app to remove all locally stored information
- **Clear History**: Delete individual scan history items or clear all history within the app
- **Camera Access**: Revoke camera permission in iOS Settings > Dropp
- **Photo Access**: Managed by iOS per-photo permissions
- **Subscription**: Cancel anytime through iOS Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on our website with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Data Retention

- Local data persists until you delete it or uninstall the app
- Dropp's app functions do not write a personal profile or search history to its product catalog
- External service providers may process network metadata, such as IP address and connection logs, under their own policies
- Scan history can be cleared at any time from within the app

## International Users

Dropp is available worldwide. Since all data is stored locally on your device and we don't collect personal information, international data transfer regulations (such as GDPR) related to personal data processing do not apply to our data handling practices.

However, we respect user privacy regardless of location and uphold privacy standards consistent with:
- EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
- Other international privacy frameworks

## Third-Party Services

Dropp uses the following third-party services:

### OpenFoodFacts
- Purpose: Product information database
- Data shared: Barcode numbers and product-search terms used to retrieve public product data
- Privacy Policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy-policy

### Convex
- Purpose: Dropp ownership catalog and product/brand search service
- Data shared: Voluntary search terms or current product details, selected market, country filters, and movement preferences needed for a requested result
- Privacy Policy: https://www.convex.dev/legal/privacy

### Apple App Store
- Purpose: App distribution and in-app purchase processing
- Data shared: None directly; Apple processes payments independently
- Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
