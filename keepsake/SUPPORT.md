# Keepsake Support

Keepsake saves the photos and videos from your memories data export to your iPhone or iPad,
with their original dates, locations and quality. Everything happens on your device.

## Getting Started

1. In your account settings, open **My Data** and request an export that includes **Memories** and **JSON files**.
2. When the export is ready, download the ZIP file (or files) from the email you receive and save them to the Files app.
3. Open Keepsake, tap **Continue**, then **Choose ZIP File** and select your export.
4. Review your memories in the gallery and save the ones you want, or tap **Select** to save everything at once.

Saved memories go to a **Keepsake** album in Photos (you can turn this off before saving).

## Tips

- **Use a fresh export.** Media links inside an export can expire after a few days, so a newly downloaded export works best.
- **Multiple ZIP files?** Add them all on the selection screen, then process them together.
- **Large libraries** can take a while to process. Keep the app open until processing finishes.

## Troubleshooting

**"No photos or videos found"**
- Make sure you selected the export ZIP that includes Memories and JSON files.
- Request a new export if the one you have is older than a few days.

**Some memories failed to save**
- Check that Keepsake has Photos access: Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Keepsake.
- Make sure your device has enough free storage, then try again.
- Open the results banner in the gallery to see and share a report of the failed files.

**Purchase didn't unlock unlimited saves**
- Tap **Unlock unlimited** on the first screen, then **Restore Purchase**.
- Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

## Privacy
