# Keepsake Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 12, 2026

## Overview

Keepsake: Memories Saver ("the App") helps you back up and preserve the memories in a data
export you download yourself, saving your photos and videos in the correct order, with the
right dates and original quality.

## Key Principles

**What we do**
- **On-device processing.** ZIP analysis, media processing and storage happen on your device.
- **No developer servers.** We never upload your memories to servers operated by us.
- **No account required.**
- **No data collection.** We don't collect personal data, usage statistics or analytics.
- **No tracking.** No tracking pixels, cookies or analytics tools.

**What we don't do**
- We don't sell or share your data.
- We don't use your data for advertising.
- We don't collect location information or device identifiers beyond what iOS requires.

## Data Handling

### Your Memories
When you import a data export ZIP file:
1. The ZIP is processed entirely on your device.
2. Media included in the ZIP is extracted locally. Media that the export represents as a
   download link is fetched directly from the export's own provider link to your device.
3. No copies are sent to servers operated by us.
4. All data remains under your control.

### App Files
The app stores extracted memories and your preferences in its private, sandboxed storage on
your device. You can delete them by deleting memories in the app, or by deleting the app
(Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Keepsake > Delete App).

### No Account Access
- The app does not sign in to or authenticate with any social network or online account.
- We never ask for or store your credentials.
- Network access is used only to fetch provider-hosted media links contained in your own
  export, and for Apple services when you make an in-app purchase.

## In-App Purchase

The optional one-time "Unlimited Saves" purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never see
your payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks that the purchase occurred.

## iOS Permissions

- **Files:** read-only access to the ZIP files you explicitly select.
- **Photos:** used only to save memories to your library when you choose to save them.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Data Deletion

Delete individual memories in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data. Deleted
data cannot be recovered.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes take effect when posted here.
